User Guide

Getting Started

Welcome to Assistant AI! Follow these simple steps to get started:

  1. Register for a new account by clicking on the Register link on the homepage.
  2. Log in using your credentials.
  3. Explore the features available on your dashboard.
Features Overview

Assistant AI offers a range of features to assist you:

  • Chatbot: Engage in conversation with our AI-powered chatbot for instant support.
  • Calculator: Use the built-in calculator for quick calculations.
  • Notepad: Save your notes and ideas securely.
  • Language Translator: Translate text into multiple languages with ease.
  • Settings: Customize your profile and preferences.
Troubleshooting

If you encounter any issues while using Assistant AI, consider the following steps:

  1. Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
  2. Clear your browser cache and cookies.
  3. If the issue persists, check our Help Center for common solutions.
